A booth loop that runs all day.

Product videos, demo times and slides on the booth TV, looping from setup to teardown, even when the show-floor Wi-Fi doesn’t.

Download freeOpen in your browser

Set it up once, then work the booth

A booth TV should catch eyes from the aisle without anyone babysitting it. Put your product video, demo schedule and a couple of slides in a folder, and LoopCast loops them fullscreen on the TV for as long as the show runs.

  • Video and slides together. MP4 sizzle reels, PNG or JPG slides, and PDF one-pagers in the same loop.
  • No internet needed. Show-floor Wi-Fi is slow and crowded. LoopCast plays from your laptop.
  • Keeps the screen awake so the laptop doesn’t sleep in the middle of the afternoon.
  • One screen is fine. If the TV is the only display, Fullscreen here fills it, and Esc brings the controls back.

Live demos on the big screen

Plug a demo laptop or camera into a USB HDMI capture stick and it appears under Inputs. Cut to the live demo, then press play to go back to the loop.
