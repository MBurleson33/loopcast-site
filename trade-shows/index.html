Set it up once, then work the booth

A booth TV should catch eyes from the aisle without anyone babysitting it. Put your product video, demo schedule and a couple of slides in a folder, and LoopCast loops them fullscreen on the TV for as long as the show runs.

  • Video and slides together. MP4 sizzle reels, PNG or JPG slides, and PDF one-pagers in the same loop.
  • No internet needed. Show-floor Wi-Fi is slow and crowded. LoopCast plays from your laptop.
  • Keeps the screen awake so the laptop doesn’t sleep in the middle of the afternoon.
  • One screen is fine. If the TV is the only display, Fullscreen here fills it, and Esc brings the controls back.

Live demos on the big screen

Plug a demo laptop or camera into a USB HDMI capture stick and it appears under Inputs. Cut to the live demo, then press play to go back to the loop. Or keep the loop going and put the camera in a corner with picture in picture.

Collect leads from the aisle

Press Q and paste your demo booking or brochure link, and a QR code sits in the corner of every slide. QR codes on screen.

Loop a product video all day

Most booths run one sizzle reel or product video on repeat. Put the video file in a folder, choose the folder in LoopCast, and press play. When the video ends it starts again, all day, with no buffering and no “up next” screens like a streaming site would show.

  • Export MP4 (H.264) at 1920×1080. It plays smoothly on any laptop and fills a standard booth TV with no black bars.
  • Several videos? Put them all in the folder. LoopCast plays them in order and loops back to the first. Name them 01, 02, 03 to set the order.
  • Mix in slides between videos: a demo schedule, a special offer or your booth number. Each slide stays up for the time you set.
  • Sound: video audio plays through the TV or laptop. Show floors are loud, so most booths turn the TV down or export a version without sound, with captions burned in.
  • Make the first seconds count. People glance for two or three seconds as they walk by. Put your logo and what you do on screen right away, not after a long intro.

What to bring

  • A laptop (Windows 10 or 11, or a Mac on macOS 12 or later) with LoopCast installed and your folder copied onto it. Don’t rely on a cloud folder at the venue.
  • An HDMI cable long enough to reach the TV, plus a USB-C to HDMI adapter if your laptop needs one.
  • The laptop’s power cable and a power strip. A loop that runs eight hours will drain a battery.
  • A copy of the folder on a USB stick, in case you need to run it from another computer.

On the show floor

  1. Connect the TV and set it as a second screen (Windows key + P, then Extend), or use it as the only screen.
  2. Open LoopCast, choose the folder and press Show on projector, or Fullscreen here if the TV is the only display.
  3. Press play. Keep the laptop lid open unless you know your laptop keeps running with the lid closed and a screen attached.
  4. Between demos, press Logo (H) for a clean branded screen, or Black (B) during a talk.

Questions

How do I loop a video on a TV at a trade show?

Put the video file (MP4 works best) in a folder on a laptop, connect the laptop to the TV with HDMI, open LoopCast, choose the folder and press play. It repeats until you stop it.

Do I need Wi-Fi at the booth?

No. LoopCast plays from your laptop and works offline, so slow or crowded show-floor Wi-Fi doesn’t matter.

Will the laptop go to sleep during the show?

Not while the LoopCast desktop app is playing; it keeps the computer awake. Keep the laptop plugged in.

Can I play a video and slides in the same loop?

Yes. Put MP4 videos, JPG or PNG slides and PDFs in the same folder. Videos play to the end; slides stay up for the time you set.

Can I show a QR code for leads?

Yes. Press Q, paste the link for booking a demo or downloading a brochure, and add a few words. The code stays in the corner over every slide and video until you hide it.
