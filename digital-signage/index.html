Simple digital signage for lobbies, offices and schools.

Today’s events, welcome messages, menus and announcements on a lobby or hallway TV. Point LoopCast at a folder and it loops, with no subscription and no cloud account.

Download freeOpen in your browser

Any computer and any TV

Digital signage doesn’t need special players or monthly fees. Connect a computer to the TV, open LoopCast, choose a folder, and it loops your images, PDFs and videos fullscreen. If the TV is the only screen, set Settings › Projector to This screen and LoopCast fills it every time.

  • Schools: hallway and cafeteria screens with this week’s news, schedules and spirit days.
  • Offices and lobbies: today’s meetings and rooms, visitor welcome messages, wayfinding.
  • Waiting rooms and front desks: services, hours and reminders.
  • Restaurants and cafés: menu boards and specials as images or a PDF.

Update it from the folder

LoopCast checks the folder every time a playlist comes around, so changing the sign is as simple as replacing a file. Put the folder in a shared drive or a synced cloud folder and anyone on your team can update it.
