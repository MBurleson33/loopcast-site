Your license key is in your receipt email from Polar. Here’s how to use it.

Activate LoopCast

  1. Find your key. It’s in the order email from Polar and starts with LOOPCAST-. Can’t find the email? Check spam, or open the order from Polar’s customer portal using the same email address.
  2. Open LoopCast on the computer that runs your screens. Don’t have it yet? Download it for Windows or Mac.
  3. Click the trial button in the top bar, or the LoopCast logo › License.
  4. Paste your key and click Activate. It needs the internet this once; after that LoopCast works offline.

Good to know

  • One license, one computer. Moving to a new computer? On the old one, open About › License and choose Move license, then activate on the new one.
  • Old computer gone? Free the license from Polar’s customer portal (the link is in your receipt), or email us.
  • Every feature is included: playlists, countdowns, QR codes, the phone remote, picture in picture and the streaming window.
