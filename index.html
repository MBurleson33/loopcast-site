The conference screens, handled.

LoopCast plays a folder of slides and videos on the projector, on repeat, without internet. Drop in your files, press play, and get back to running the event.

Set up in the time it takes to find the HDMI cable.

No slide software to wrangle and no accounts. LoopCast works from folders you already know how to make.

Put your files in folders

Each folder becomes a playlist. Name files 01, 02, 03 to set the order. Add a logo for the in-between moments.

Show on the projector

One button opens the output fullscreen on the second display, at the projector’s own resolution. Your laptop keeps the controls.

Press play

Slides hold for as long as you choose, videos play to the end, and the whole thing loops until you stop it.

Built for the AV table, not the boardroom.

Everything you need to keep a room’s screens right all day, and nothing you’ll have to learn on the spot.

Always know what the room is seeing.

The preview on your laptop matches the projector. A red outline means it’s live, and the next slide is marked in green so there are no surprises.

Make a lunch version without touching the original.

Duplicate a playlist, hide the slides that don’t fit, and give it its own ending. The original stays exactly as it was.

Need one slide or video to stay up? Set it to repeat until you press Next.

Set it once, then leave it alone.

Choose how long slides stay up, crossfade or cut, fit or fill the screen, and whether videos play sound. LoopCast remembers your folder and settings for next time.
