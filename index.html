Questions, answered.

Anything else? The browser version is the fastest way to see it for yourself.

Does it need internet?

No. LoopCast runs entirely on your computer and plays files from a folder on it. Internet is only needed to download it.

Will it work with our projector or TV?

Yes, if your computer sees it as a second display. LoopCast opens the output fullscreen on that display at its own resolution, whether it’s a projector, a TV or an LED wall processor.

What kinds of files can I use?

Images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG) and video (MP4 and WebM). Export slides from PowerPoint, Keynote or Canva as images and drop them in a folder.

Can I change slides during the event?

Yes. Add or remove files in the folder and LoopCast picks up the changes the next time the playlist comes around, or right away if you press Rescan.

Can I show a presenter’s laptop or a camera?

Yes. Plug it into an inexpensive USB HDMI capture stick and it shows up under Inputs. Preview it, then put it on screen with one click. Your playlist waits underneath, and Logo and Black still work.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes, on macOS 12 or later, on Intel and Apple Silicon. The first time you open it, allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Can I use it without installing anything?

Yes. Open in your browser runs the full player in Chrome or Edge. The desktop app adds a few conveniences: it opens the projector window fullscreen on its own, where the browser needs one click or the F key.

Why does my computer warn me when I install it?

LoopCast isn’t signed with a paid developer certificate yet, so Windows and macOS ask you to confirm the first time. On Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac use Open Anyway in Privacy & Security.
