Also built in

The little things that save an event.

Click through on cue

Set a playlist to Manual and each slide waits for you. Arrow keys, Page Down or a presentation clicker move it on.

PDFs become slides

Every page of a PDF plays as its own slide, sharp at the projector’s resolution. Export PowerPoint or Keynote decks as PDFs.

Repeat one file

Keep a single image or video up until you press next, while the rest of the playlist waits.

Copies with hidden files

Duplicate a playlist and hide a few files for the second session. The original stays untouched.

End on your logo

Each playlist can loop, stop on the last slide, or finish on your logo screen by itself.

Live web pages

Paste a web address into a playlist to show a live poll, social wall or schedule. In Manual mode you can click and scroll it from your laptop.

Send from a phone

Scan a QR code and send photos, videos or PDFs from a phone on the same Wi-Fi. They land in a playlist, ready when you are.

One screen is enough

No projector? Fullscreen here fills the computer you’re on, and Esc brings the controls back.
