Put a slideshow on a lobby TV from a laptop.

A lobby TV should show something useful all day without anyone babysitting it. A spare laptop or a small PC and a free app are enough, with no subscription and no cloud account.

The setup

  1. Place the computer near the TV and connect it with HDMI (or a USB-C to HDMI adapter). Plug it into power.
  2. Choose how the TV is used. If the computer also has its own screen, extend the display (Windows + P, then Extend; on a Mac, System Settings › Displays). If the TV is the only screen, that’s fine too.
  3. Make a folder called something like “Lobby” and put your pictures, videos and PDFs in it.
  4. Open LoopCast, choose the folder, press Show on projector, then press play.

Make it start by itself

A lobby sign should come back on its own after a power cut or a Monday-morning restart. In the LoopCast desktop app:

  1. Set Settings › Projector to the TV, or to This screen if the TV is the only display.
  2. Turn on Start the show automatically. LoopCast opens the projector and plays the last playlist as soon as it opens.
  3. Turn on Open LoopCast when this computer starts.

Together, the screen recovers by itself after a restart. If the computer asks for a password at startup, set it to sign in automatically (only on a computer that stays in a safe place) or the sign will wait at the login screen.

Change it by time of day

In Settings › Schedule, add times and a playlist for each: a Morning playlist at 7:30 with today’s events, Lunch at 11:30 with the menu, Evening at 5:00 with tomorrow’s schedule. LoopCast switches by itself using the computer’s clock.

Keep it running all day

  • The desktop app keeps the computer awake while it plays. In a browser, set the computer’s sleep to never.
  • Turn off notifications during opening hours (Focus on Windows and Mac) so pop-ups don’t appear on the TV if it’s the only screen.
  • Schedule system updates for after hours so the computer doesn’t restart at noon.
  • Turn off the TV’s own sleep or eco timer. Many TVs switch off when they don’t see a remote press for a few hours.

Update it without interrupting

Drop new files into the folder, or delete old ones. LoopCast picks up the changes the next time the loop comes around, so the screen never goes blank while you edit.

Update it from your desk: make the Lobby folder a synced cloud folder, like OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox, on the lobby computer. Anyone with access can add this week’s slides from their own computer, and they appear on the TV once the folder syncs. LoopCast itself still plays from the computer, so the sign keeps running if the internet drops.

What to show

  • Today’s events and rooms, the Wi-Fi name and password, and a welcome message.
  • Upcoming dates, staff photos or a short video about your organization.
  • A live web page, like a calendar or dashboard, in the desktop app (web pages need internet).
  • Your logo between messages. Name an image logo.png and press H to show it any time.
  • The time in a corner: press C, or turn on the clock in Settings.

Which computer?

Any Windows 10 or 11 computer or Mac running macOS 12 or later will do. An old laptop with its lid open works. For a neat install behind the TV, a small mini PC is tidy and quiet. Use a wired network connection if you want the folder to sync reliably.

More ideas for lobbies, offices and schools are on the digital signage page.

Questions

Can the lobby TV start the slideshow by itself in the morning?

Yes. In the desktop app, turn on Start the show automatically and Open LoopCast when this computer starts. When the computer starts, LoopCast opens and plays the last playlist on the TV.

Can I update the lobby TV from another computer?

Yes. Make the folder LoopCast plays a synced cloud folder (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox). New files appear on the TV after they sync.

Can it show different slides in the morning and afternoon?

Yes. In Settings › Schedule, add times and the playlist for each. LoopCast switches by itself.

Do I need a subscription?

No. LoopCast is free during our launch and doesn’t need an account or a cloud service.

What if the TV is the only screen?

Set Settings › Projector to This screen and LoopCast fills it every time.
