Set up a church announcement loop.

A weekly routine for pre-service announcements that takes ten minutes to prepare and one click on Sunday, run by whoever is on the rota that week.

Download freeOpen in your browser

During the week: make the slides

  1. Design this week’s announcements in Canva, PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides at 16:9 (1920×1080).
  2. Export them as a PDF, or as images. Each page of a PDF becomes its own slide in LoopCast.
  3. Put the file in a folder called Announcements. Add a short video or two if you have them.

Keep each slide to one announcement: a headline, a date and one line of detail. If people need more, add a QR code (see below) instead of more words.

Organize by moment

Make one folder per moment, and each becomes a playlist you can switch between with a click:

  • Welcome: the loop people see as they walk in: a welcome, coffee and kids’ check-in.
  • Announcements: this week’s slides.
  • Countdown: a countdown video, set to stop when it ends, if you use one.
  • After: a goodbye loop with next week’s events.

Need a version for the second service without one announcement? Duplicate the playlist and hide that slide. The original stays the same.

On Sunday: one click

  1. Open LoopCast on the computer connected to the projector or lobby TV.
  2. Press Show on projector, then play the Welcome playlist.
  3. Five minutes out, press Timer (T) and type the start time, like 10:30, for a full-screen “Starting in” countdown. It goes back to the slides at zero.
  4. When the service starts, press Logo (H) to cover the screen with your church logo, or Black (B).

Add a QR code for giving or sign-ups

Press QR (Q), paste your giving page or connection card link, and type “Scan to give”. The code sits in a corner over every slide until you press Q again, so you don’t need to put it on any slides. More about QR codes on screen.

A quick message during the service

A car with its lights on, a parent needed in the nursery: press Message (M), type a line, and it shows along the bottom of the screen over whatever is up. Press M again to clear it. For a discreet nursery call, a short code like “Nursery: 214” works well.

The lobby and kids’ check-in screens

Run a copy of LoopCast on each screen’s computer, each with its own folder. A lobby TV can open LoopCast and start the loop by itself every Sunday morning: turn on Start the show automatically and Open LoopCast when this computer starts in Settings, then use Settings › Schedule to switch playlists at set times.

On the livestream too

If you stream your service, the desktop app’s streaming window sends the same announcement loop and countdown into OBS, vMix or Ecamm, so online viewers see what the room sees before the service starts.

Next week

Replace last week’s PDF with this week’s. LoopCast picks it up the next time the loop comes around, so there’s nothing to rebuild. If your team shares a cloud folder, whoever makes the slides can drop them straight into the Announcements folder from home.

Make it volunteer-proof

  • Tape a card by the computer: “Open LoopCast › Show on projector › play Welcome. T for countdown. H for logo.”
  • Name folders by what they’re for, not by date.
  • Keep a logo.png in the main folder so H always has something to show.
  • Use the phone remote if the computer is in the booth and the volunteer is down front.

Questions

What’s the easiest way to loop church announcements?

Export your slides as a PDF, put it in a folder, open LoopCast, choose the folder, press Show on projector and play. It loops until you stop it.

Can I add a countdown before the service?

Yes. Press T and type the start time, like 10:30, for a full-screen countdown that goes back to the slides at zero. A countdown video works too.

Will it replace ProPresenter or our worship software?

No. LoopCast is for the loops around the service: announcements, welcome slides and lobby screens. Keep your lyric software for worship. How they compare.

Can I show a giving QR code?

Yes. Press Q, paste the link and add “Scan to give”. It shows in a corner over every slide until you hide it.

Is it free for churches?

Yes. LoopCast is free during our launch, for churches and everyone else.
