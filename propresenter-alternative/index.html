LoopCast vs ProPresenter.

ProPresenter is a full production suite. LoopCast does one job: keeping announcements, slides and video looping on a screen. Here’s an honest look at when each one is the right tool.

The short answer

If you run worship with lyrics, scripture, stage displays and multiple outputs, use ProPresenter (or another worship app). It’s built for that and LoopCast isn’t.

If a screen only needs to loop, like a lobby TV, a pre-service announcement loop, a kids’ check-in screen, a conference walk-in or a booth display, LoopCast is free, quicker to learn, and doesn’t need a subscription seat for every computer.

What ProPresenter does that LoopCast doesn’t

ProPresenter is professional presentation software for worship and live production. It handles song lyrics and scripture, stage displays for musicians and speakers, multiple screens with different content, timeline cues, and broadcast integrations. If your Sunday depends on those, it’s the right tool, and plenty of churches consider it the standard.

What LoopCast does well

  • Loops images, PDFs and video from folders, one folder per playlist. Update the folder and the loop updates.
  • A live preview of the screen, with the current and next item marked.
  • Logo screen and fade to black on one key.
  • Per-playlist endings (loop, stop or end on the logo), per-item repeat, and a manual mode for clickers.
  • Countdown to a time, a clock, a message bar and a QR code overlay.
  • Schedules that switch playlists at set times, and starting by itself when the computer starts, for unattended screens.
  • Runs offline on Windows and Mac, or in any modern browser.

Using both

The two work well side by side. Keep ProPresenter on the main sanctuary computer for worship, and put LoopCast on a second computer for the lobby TV, the kids’ check-in screen and the overflow room. The volunteers who run those screens get one simple app, and your ProPresenter operator isn’t also managing announcements.

Cost over a year

For one computer, ProPresenter’s listed annual price is $289. A church with a sanctuary computer plus two lobby or classroom screens would need three seats for ProPresenter; LoopCast on the two extra screens costs nothing during our launch. Questions

Is LoopCast a replacement for ProPresenter?

Only for loops. LoopCast plays announcements, slides and video on repeat. It doesn’t do lyrics, scripture, stage displays or multi-screen production, so churches that need those should keep ProPresenter or another worship app.

How much does ProPresenter cost?

As of November 2026, Renewed Vision lists ProPresenter at $29 a month or $289 a year for one seat, with discounts for additional annual seats. New users can only buy it as a subscription.

Can I use LoopCast and ProPresenter together?

Yes. Many churches run ProPresenter for worship and LoopCast on a second computer for the lobby, kids’ check-in or pre-service screens, so the main operator isn’t tied up with announcements.

Is LoopCast free?

Yes, LoopCast is free during our launch, on Windows, Mac and in the browser.

Does LoopCast work offline like ProPresenter?

Yes. LoopCast plays files from your computer and needs no internet.
