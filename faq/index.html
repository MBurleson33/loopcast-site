Questions, answered.

Everything people usually ask before their first event with LoopCast.

Download freeOpen in your browser
Is LoopCast free?

Yes. You can download LoopCast for Windows and Mac, or open it in your browser, at no cost.

Does it need internet?

No. LoopCast runs on your computer and plays files from a folder on it. You only need internet to download it.

Will it work with our projector or TV?

Yes, if your computer sees it as a second display: a projector, a TV, or an LED wall processor over HDMI, DisplayPort or USB-C. LoopCast opens the output fullscreen on it at its own resolution.

What if I only have one screen?

Use Fullscreen here (the expand button, or the F key). LoopCast fills the screen it runs on, and Esc brings back the controls. If LoopCast only finds one display, Show on projector does this for you.

What kinds of files can I use?

Images (JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP, SVG), PDFs, and video (MP4, WebM, MOV). Each page of a PDF becomes its own slide. MP4 with H.264 is the safest choice for video.

Can it play PowerPoint or Keynote files?

Not directly. Save your deck as a PDF (in PowerPoint, File › Export › PDF; in Keynote, File › Export To › PDF) and put the PDF in the folder. If LoopCast finds a PowerPoint or Keynote file, it tells you how to export it.

Can I click through slides myself?

Yes. Set a playlist’s Advance option to Manual and each slide stays up until you press next: the arrow keys, Page Up and Page Down, or a presentation clicker. Click any slide to jump straight to it. Videos play once and hold on their last frame.

Can I add or swap files during an event?

Yes. Add or remove files in the folder and LoopCast picks up the changes the next time the playlist comes around, or right away if you press Rescan.

Can I show a presenter’s laptop or a camera?

Yes. Plug it into a USB HDMI capture stick and it shows up under Inputs. Preview it, then put it on screen with one click. The playlist waits underneath, and Logo and Black still work.

Does it work on a Mac?

Yes, on macOS 12 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. The first time you open it, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Can I use it without installing anything?

Yes. Open in your browser runs the full player in any modern browser. The desktop app adds a few conveniences, like opening the projector window fullscreen on its own.

Why does my computer warn me when I install it?

LoopCast isn’t signed with a paid developer certificate yet. Without one, Windows and macOS can’t confirm who made the app, so they ask you to confirm the first time. The warning is about an unverified publisher; it doesn’t mean the app was found to be harmful. Download it only from loopcast.space. The Installing LoopCast page explains each step. On Windows choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, go to System Settings › Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

How do I get help or send feedback?

Email hello@loopcast.space. Include your version number (click the LoopCast logo in the app to see it) and whether you use Windows, Mac or the browser version.

How do I show a logo between sessions?

Put an image named logo.png in the main media folder. Press Logo (or H) to cover the screen with it while the playlist keeps its place underneath. A playlist can also end on the logo by itself.
