The announcement loop, without tying up the presentation computer

Most churches loop announcements before the service and between services. LoopCast does that one job well: point it at a folder of announcement slides and it plays them on repeat, fullscreen on the sanctuary projector or the lobby TV.

  • Volunteer-friendly. Choose the folder, click Show on projector, press play. Nothing to configure.
  • Announcements as a PDF. Export this week’s slides from Canva, PowerPoint or Keynote as a PDF and drop it in. Each page becomes a slide.
  • A countdown to the start time. Press T, type 10:30, and the screen counts down, then goes back to the slides at zero. Countdown videos work too.
  • Lobby and kids’ check-in screens. Run a separate copy on each screen’s computer, each with its own folder.

A Sunday with LoopCast

  1. 8:30 The lobby TV starts by itself and loops the welcome playlist: coffee, kids’ check-in, this week’s events.
  2. 10:00 On the sanctuary screen, a volunteer plays the Announcements playlist, with a giving QR code in the corner.
  3. 10:25 T, then 10:30: a full-screen “Starting in” countdown on the projector and the livestream.
  4. 10:30 H for the church logo as worship begins, or hand over to your worship software.
  5. 11:45 The goodbye loop with next week’s events while people leave.

Update it in seconds

Replace last week’s files with this week’s and LoopCast picks them up the next time the loop comes around. Want a version for the second service without one announcement? Duplicate the playlist and hide that slide. The original stays as it was. If the person making the slides works from home, a shared cloud folder lets them drop the file straight in.

Works alongside your worship software

LoopCast doesn’t replace lyric and sermon software. It’s for the loops around the service, on the same projector before worship starts or on a different screen entirely. If you use ProPresenter, FreeShow or OpenLP for worship, LoopCast can run the lobby and kids’ screens on other computers so your main operator can focus on the service. Lobby and kids’ screens that run themselves

In the desktop app, turn on Start the show automatically and Open LoopCast when this computer starts. Turn the computer on Sunday morning and the screen comes up with your welcome loop. Use Settings › Schedule to switch to the after-service loop at noon.

Questions

Is LoopCast free for churches?

Yes. LoopCast is free during our launch, on Windows, Mac and in the browser.

Does it replace ProPresenter or our lyrics software?

No. LoopCast is for the loops around the service. Keep your worship software for lyrics and scripture, and use LoopCast for announcements, the lobby and kids’ screens.

Can a volunteer run it?

Yes. Choose the folder, press Show on projector and play. H shows the logo, B goes to black and T starts a countdown.

Can we show a countdown to the service?

Yes. Press T and type the start time, like 10:30. The countdown covers the screen and goes back to the slides at zero.

Can it show a QR code for online giving?

Yes. Press Q, paste the giving link and add “Scan to give”. It sits in a corner over every slide until you hide it.

Can it run the lobby TV by itself?

Yes. In the desktop app, turn on Start the show automatically and Open LoopCast when this computer starts, and use Settings › Schedule to switch playlists at set times.
